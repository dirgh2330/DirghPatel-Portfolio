1.0 Purpose

This policy explains what information this website collects when a person visits, why it is collected, and how it is handled. It is written so that any visitor can understand their privacy at a glance, and it reflects a privacy by design approach where the least amount of data is collected to operate the site.

2.0 Scope

This policy covers the personal portfolio website of Dirgh Patel, hosted at dirgh2330.github.io, including the analytics used to measure traffic, the contact form, and any embedded video content. It does not cover external websites that this site links to, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

3.0 Information Collected

3.1 Website analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy friendly service that does not use cookies and does not track visitors across other websites. The information gathered is aggregate only and cannot identify a person. It includes the number of visits, which pages are viewed, the country a visit comes from, the type of device used, and the website or link that referred the visit, such as a search engine or a social network.

3.2 Contact form

If a visitor chooses to use the contact form, the name, email address, and contact detail entered are transmitted to the site owner's email inbox through a third party form service. This information is provided voluntarily by the visitor and is used only to respond to the message.

3.3 Embedded video

The projects section includes video research hosted on YouTube. Videos load only after a visitor clicks to play one, and they use YouTube's privacy enhanced player, which avoids setting tracking cookies until playback begins.

4.0 Information Not Collected

  • This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies.
  • It does not build a behavioural profile of any visitor.
  • It does not store visitor IP addresses for the purpose of tracking individuals.
  • It does not attempt to identify individual visitors through analytics.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

5.0 Lawful Basis and Consent

Website analytics on this site are aggregate and cookie less, so they do not rely on consent for individual tracking, because no individual tracking takes place. Information submitted through the contact form is collected only with the visitor's active participation, since the visitor chooses to type and send it. By using the contact form, a visitor consents to that information being used to reply to them.

6.0 Data Use, Retention, and Sharing

Analytics data is used only to understand how the site is found and used, so that it can be improved. Contact form submissions are used only to reply to the sender and are retained no longer than needed for that correspondence. The only third parties involved are the analytics provider and the form delivery service, each acting solely to provide their stated function. No information from this site is used for marketing or sold to anyone.

7.0 Visitor Rights and Choices

Because no cookies or personal identifiers are used for analytics, there is nothing a visitor needs to opt out of for normal browsing. A visitor who has sent a message through the contact form may ask, at any time, what information was received, request a copy of it, or request that it be deleted. Requests can be made using the contact details in section 9.0.

Privacy by Design

This site was built to collect the minimum information required to function. Analytics are cookie less by default and video players are loaded only on request, so a typical visit leaves no personal trace.

8.0 Policy Review

This policy is reviewed at least once a year, and sooner if the way the site collects or uses information changes. The version number and effective date in the document control table above are updated whenever a change is made.

9.0 Contact

Questions about this policy, or about information sent through the contact form, can be directed to the document owner.
