Who I am

I am an IT and security professional based in Toronto with about two years across an internal help desk and a security operations center. My day to day has run from resolving hardware, software, and access tickets for a full team to monitoring a SIEM and triaging alerts against MITRE ATT&CK.

That mix means I am comfortable on both sides of the desk: I can set up a new hire, reset access, and patch a fleet, and I can also investigate a suspicious alert, confirm whether it is real, and escalate it through containment and recovery.
